## I bought a Garmin smartwatch, but Garmin Connect wouldn't recognize it

I recently bought a Garmin Instinct 3, a smartwatch for running. I unboxed it and immediately tried pairing it with my phone, but Garmin Connect just wouldn't recognize the device. It showed up fine in my phone's Bluetooth list, yet the Garmin Connect app could never find it.

## I tried every official troubleshooting step

The official support pages listed plenty of troubleshooting steps — restarting, factory reset, and so on — but no matter what I tried, the result was the same. Thinking it might be a problem with my phone, I dug out an old, unused phone and tried again, with the same result. I even switched from Android to iPhone, and still nothing changed.

On top of that, the firmware that shipped from the factory was version 12, a bit outdated, so I connected the watch to my computer via USB and tried updating it through Garmin Express. Still, nothing changed.

## Support gave me an answer I never would have guessed

I contacted Garmin support via chat and explained the whole situation, and they gave me a reason that's documented nowhere: "You can't register the device because your country of residence isn't supported for that device's SKU." I'd owned another Garmin device before, so I already had a Garmin Connect account. Apparently, because I'd registered that account with the US as my country of residence, my account was still set to the US. It turns out the Garmin Instinct 3 I'd just bought, while sold in the US, apparently has a Japan-specific SKU that supports Suica (Japan's transit IC card). Because of that, a user registered as living in the US (I live in Japan now though, that's why I got the Japanese version) who tries to select and connect the Instinct 3 just won't get a connection. Once I changed my country of residence to Japan in the settings and repeated the same steps, my phone recognized the device immediately, no issues at all.

I doubt many people run into this exact edge case, but I'm leaving this here as a record, just in case it helps someone who hits the same wall.
